## 2. Passasjer som bærer påstanden (ordrett)

Abstract (s. 118):
> «This article examines how Artificial Intelligence (AI) can transform intralingual translation, with a focus on historical varieties, particularly Early Modern English. … the study investigates the modernization of Early Modern English, using literary and specialized texts to demonstrate AI's effectiveness in handling complex vocabulary and syntax. The paper also evaluates AI's impact on fields like historical linguistics and digital humanities, discussing both the benefits and the limitations, such as the risk of anachronism and the need for human oversight.»

Fotnote 1 (s. 118) — skillet vi selv gjør i moderniseringen:
> «… I distinguish between spelling normalization and language modernization as two distinct processes, one concerning only the Early Modern English spelling and the other working with language as a whole, meaning also modernizing morphosyntactic and lexical structures.»

## 3. Hva kilden IKKE sier

- Empirisk grunnlag er **VARD2 (regelbasert) og ChatGPT** på **engelsk** (Early Modern English); ingen CER, ingen norsk, intet ö/ø.
- Et **teoretisk rammeverk med eksempler**, ikke en systematisk evaluering; fremhever selv «risk of anachronism and the need for human oversight».
- Nevner **ikke** sikkerhetsfiltre, apparat eller referansefri QE. Verdien for oss er skillet normalisering vs. modernisering (som vår «modernisering på belegg» opererer i), ikke en metodemåling.
